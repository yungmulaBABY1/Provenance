  Revision 2 (cn7-map-2): every SCORED row tags each inference (+) supporting concern or (-)

REVISION 2 — DECISIONS TAKEN (2026-11-04; the five review positions plus three additions)
 1. Mapping explicit and versioned: cn7-map-2, declared in the output (MAP_VERSION_MISSING,
    MAP_VERSION_MISMATCH). CN7-R7.
 2. Unscreened co-citation: never the concern basis for uptake or neglect; review beside
    qualifying evidence; insufficient alone. Direction tags close the gap where the other
    evidence points against the concern. CN7-R7, CN7-R10.
 3. Bounded negatives: allowed within the screened results, never beyond the search. CN7-R6.
 4. Empty adjudication corpus: only when no evidence-base claim is made; otherwise a SEARCHED,
    locked corpus. CN7-R1, CN7-R7.
 5. 70% gate: unchanged and provisional; coverage sensitivity reported; band enforcement after
    Gate B. CN7-R7.
 6. Added: passage locators for INSPECTED rows. CN7-R3, CN7-R4.
 7. Added: the SCORED 0 hard rule is enforced through (-) evidence. CN7-R7.
 8. Added: rule ablation script, tests/ablate-citation-network-v2-gates.py.
